About us

A small studio with a big soft spot for growing brands

We're Pixel Parlour: strategists, designers and writers who love helping independent businesses get the attention they deserve, without the jargon, pressure or corporate stiffness.

Our story

Why Pixel Parlour exists

Placeholder copy

Pixel Parlour started with a simple belief: small and growing businesses deserve marketing that's as thoughtful as the work they put into their own products and services. Too often, owners are left choosing between agencies that feel cold and expensive, or doing it all themselves late at night.

So we built the kind of studio we'd want to hire: warm, honest and genuinely invested in our clients' success. We take time to understand your business, explain things in plain English and celebrate your wins as if they were our own.

Whether you're launching something new or ready for your next stage of growth, we'd love to hear your story and help you write the next chapter.

Our values

What we care about

These aren't posters on a wall. They're how we make decisions every day.

  • Curiosity

    We ask lots of questions, dig into the data and stay hungry to learn what really works for your audience.

  • Honesty

    Straight answers, realistic promises and clear reporting, even when the news isn't what we hoped.

  • Craft

    We sweat the details, from the first headline to the final pixel, because quality is what people remember.

  • Results

    Pretty is lovely, but performance pays the bills. Everything we do is tied to goals you care about.

  • Kindness

    We treat clients, their customers and each other with patience and respect. It's simply better that way.

  • Fun

    Marketing should feel exciting, not exhausting. We bring energy, ideas and good humor to every project.
