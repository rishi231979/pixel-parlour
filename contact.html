Before you get in touch

Quick answers

Is the consultation really free?

Yes, completely. It's a relaxed conversation to understand your business and goals. There's no obligation and no hard sell, and you'll come away with a few useful ideas either way.

Do you work with businesses like mine?

We mainly work with small and mid-size businesses and founders across a range of industries. If you're not sure we're the right fit, just ask. We'll be honest, and if someone else would serve you better, we'll say so.

What should I prepare for the call?

Nothing formal. It helps to have a rough idea of your goals, who your ideal customers are, what you've tried before and a ballpark budget. Links to your website and social profiles are a bonus.

How soon will I hear back?

Within 1 business day. If your enquiry is urgent, mention it in your message or give us a call and we'll do our best to help sooner.
