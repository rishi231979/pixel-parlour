FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Something else on your mind? Just ask us.

How much does working with Pixel Parlour cost?

It depends on what you need, so we don't do one-size-fits-all packages. After a free consultation we send a clear proposal, either a fixed monthly retainer for ongoing work or a one-off price for projects. No hidden fees, and we'll always tell you if something isn't worth the spend.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No multi-year lock-ins. Ongoing services usually start with a short initial period so there's enough time to see real results, then continue month to month. Projects like a new website or brand are quoted as a fixed scope with clear milestones.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long game. You'll often see early movement within the first few months, with more meaningful growth typically building over six to twelve months. It depends on your competition, your website's starting point and how much content we create. We'll set realistic expectations up front.

Which platforms do you run ads on?

Mainly Google Ads (Search, Shopping, YouTube and Performance Max) and Meta (Facebook and Instagram). If another platform such as LinkedIn or TikTok suits your audience better, we'll talk it through and recommend what makes sense for your budget.

How will I know if it's working?

Every month you'll get a plain-English report and a quick call to walk through it: what we did, the numbers that matter to you (leads, sales, traffic, cost per result) and what we're doing next. If you'd like a live dashboard to check in between reports, we can set that up too.

Ready to give your marketing a little more color?

Book a free, no-pressure consultation. We'll look at where you are, where you want to be and how we can help, even if that means pointing you somewhere else.
