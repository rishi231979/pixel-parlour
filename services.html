Services FAQ

Good questions to ask before you start

Can I start with just one service?

Absolutely. Plenty of clients start with one service, such as social media or a new website, and add more once they've seen how we work. We'll always recommend the next step that makes the biggest difference, not the one that makes the biggest invoice.

Do I need a big budget to work with you?

No. We work with businesses of different sizes and shape the plan around what you're comfortable investing. If we think your budget won't get results in a particular channel, we'll tell you honestly and suggest a better use for it.

Will I own my accounts, content and website?

Yes. Ad accounts, social profiles, analytics, content and your website are set up in your business's name, and we work inside them with your permission. If we ever part ways, everything stays with you.

How quickly can we get started?

Once we've agreed a proposal, we'll share a kickoff date and a short onboarding checklist (logins, brand assets, goals). Smaller projects can usually begin quickly, while bigger ones like a full rebrand need a little planning first.
